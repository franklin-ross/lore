# Session 1 — Barovia

We enter Barovia (town) from the west at night along Old Svalich road.
Misty. Full moon. Sounds of a woman crying.

Mad Mary (character): Mother of Gertrude. Tells us it's not safe
outside. Has a doll with "Is no fun, is no Blinsky." Her daughter
was smitten with a fancy, creepy guy: Strahd.

Gertrude (character): Mad Mary's daughter. Missing; smitten with
and probably taken by Strahd.

Find Gertrude (quest): Given by Mad Mary in Barovia (town). Find
her daughter, probably at Castle Ravenloft.

Blood on the Vine Tavern (location): In Barovia (town). Seen better
days. Owned by Vistani; run by Aryk. No beer, all wheat gets eaten;
only wine from Wizard of the Vines winery.

We go to the tavern. 3 Vistani are drunk and rowdy. Another man,
long blond hair, black tunic, looks at us on entry.

Ismark (character): Mayor of Barovia (town). Long blond hair, black
tunic. Worried that Strahd wants to abduct his sister Ireena.

Ireena Kolyana (character) | Ireena: Ismark's sister; ~27; pretty,
blue eyes, red scarf covering bite marks. Strahd wants to abduct
her. He fed on her but didn't turn her. Orphan, not blood related
to Ismark.

Escort Ireena (quest): Given by Ismark. Take Ireena to Valacki or
Krezk for safety because Strahd wants her.

Count Strahd von Zarovich (character) | Strahd: Vampire lord. Lives
at Castle Ravenloft. Tall, black hair, white skin, princely. Has
many spies: maybe Vistani, bats, wolves. Doesn't like sunlight
or running water; can't enter without invitation.

Castle Ravenloft (location): Strahd's castle. Ghostly procession
marches towards it from the graveyard; legend says they're ghosts
of adventurers who tried to kill Strahd.

Strahd: Showed up at the funeral with flowers for Ireena. She
refused him. Arrived in a carriage driven by Rohadin, a dusk elf.

Barovia (town): Gothic, dark, misty, rundown.

Barovia (nation): Perpetually cloudy and grey. Nobody can leave;
mist around the border.
